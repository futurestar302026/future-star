STORE POLICIES

Returns & exchanges

Return requests can be submitted from your customer account within 7 days of delivery for unused items in original condition with tags and packaging. Items showing wear, damage after delivery, or missing tags may not qualify. Contact us before sending anything back. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method after inspection; bank processing times may vary. This policy is a suggested starting point—confirm it matches your actual operations and applicable consumer law before launch.

Shipping & delivery

Orders are processed after payment confirmation. Delivery estimates depend on the destination and courier. Once dispatched, tracking details will appear in your account. Contact Future Star if tracking has not updated for several business days.

Cancellation & refunds

Cancellation may be requested before dispatch by contacting support. Refund eligibility is reviewed under the return policy and applicable law. Approved online-payment refunds are initiated to the original payment method. Cash-on-delivery or manual orders must be handled directly by support.

Privacy & account security

Account details are used to manage orders, delivery and support. Never share your password or payment credentials. For account assistance, contact futurestar302026@gmail.com.
